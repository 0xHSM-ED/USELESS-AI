# Useless AI

Welcome to **Useless AI** — an AI that was specifically designed to be completely useless.

Ask it a question. Say hello. Insult it. Talk about Minecraft. Ask it why. It doesn't matter.

It probably won't help you.

With hundreds of completely automated responses, random reactions, stupid little Easter eggs, and absolutely zero actual intelligence, Useless AI exists for one purpose:

**To waste your time.**

No fancy AI models.
No deep learning.
No useful information.
And best of all , no wasted water.

Have fun. Or don't.
